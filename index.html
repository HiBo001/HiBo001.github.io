About Me

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the State Key Laboratory of Blockchain and Data Security, Zhejiang University (advised by Prof. Lingfeng Bao), and the Hangzhou High-Tech Zone (Binjiang) Institute of Blockchain and Data Security in Hangzhou, China.

I received my Ph.D. in Data Science and Engineering from East China Normal University (ECNU) in 2025 (advised by Prof. Zhao Zhang and Prof. Cheqing Jin).

My research interests mainly lie in blockchain systems, distributed systems, and high-performance transaction processing. I am also interested in verifiable computation and trustworthy multi-agent collaboration.
